Smoking and Mental Distress in NYC Zip code

The correlation is +0.77, which is positive and strong.

For every 1 percentage point increase in smoking_pct, distress_pct goes up about 0.4912 percentage points.

The p-value is less than 0.001, which is less than 0.05, so this relationship is statistically significant.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR PEOPLE:

In plain words: In Neighborhoods where more people smoke, they also tend to have an increase in people who are struggling with mental health. Take two neighborhoods 10 percentage points apart in smoking rates — the one with the higher smoking rate is predicted to have about 4.9 percentage points more people experiencing mental distress. This shows a strong relationship between smoking and mental distress, and it is very unlikely to be due to chance alone. However, this does not mean that smoking directly causes mental distress.
