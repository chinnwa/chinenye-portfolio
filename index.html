01 / About me

Driven by science.
Grounded in empathy.

I am pursuing my Bachelor of Science in Nursing at CUNY York College, with anticipated graduation in May 2027. Nursing brings together what matters most to me: scientific understanding, compassionate service, and meaningful advocacy.

My interests center on maternal and child health, health equity, and preventing avoidable harm. Through clinical experiences and public health coursework, I am learning to approach care with curiosity, cultural humility, and a commitment to evidence-based practice.

My goal is to begin my nursing career in labor and delivery and continue growing as an advocate for families and communities.
