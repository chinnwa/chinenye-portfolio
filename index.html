About Me

I am a Nursing student expected to graduate in December 2026. I chose nursing because it combines science, compassion, and service, allowing me to make a meaningful difference in people's lives through patient advocacy while promoting the health and well-being of individuals, children, families, and communities.


I care most about maternal and child mortality, particularly among minority populations, because many of these deaths are preventable and are often linked to health disparities, inequitable access to care, and social determinants of health. I am passionate about helping improve outcomes for mothers and children through advocacy, education, and quality healthcare.


I am working toward a career as a Labor and Delivery Nurse, with plans to pursue a Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) focused on maternal-child health or midwifery. My long-term goal is to support mothers and infants through high-quality, compassionate care while helping improve maternal health outcomes.
